Artificial Intelligence

Trust Flow

Intelligent onboarding for banks & investment firms — a unified AI platform that accelerates onboarding, elevates compliance accuracy, and streamlines documentation for banks, corporate clients, investment firms, funds, and asset managers.

Contact Us

The Challenge

Financial institutions waste time and resources on:

  • Manual document handling
  • Repeated follow-ups
  • Slow investor and corporate onboarding
  • Fragmented compliance workflows
  • High regulatory pressure
  • Long turnaround times

These bottlenecks delay revenue, frustrate clients, and increase operational costs.

The Solution

Trust Flow is a NEXGEN venture that automates the entire onboarding process with AI, ensuring fast, accurate, and complete files.

Every file arrives complete, sourced, and audit-ready—without an analyst retyping a single document.

What It Includes

  • AI document extraction
  • Automated KYC/KYB
  • Beneficial ownership mapping
  • AML & sanctions screening
  • Risk scoring & case summaries
  • Digital submission & electronic signatures
  • Centralized analyst & compliance workflows

Who It Serves

  • Banks
  • Investment Firms & Asset Managers
  • Private Equity & Venture Capital Funds
  • Corporate Service Providers
  • Real Estate Funds
  • Family Offices

What Institutions Gain

  • Cost optimization
  • Faster onboarding
  • Higher compliance accuracy
  • Better investor experience
  • Standardized risk evaluation
  • Reduced operational workload

Esaal provides a receipt management solution that provides an interactive user interface that stores and manages receipts digitally, effortlessly, and seamlessly turns them into expenses, enabling you to save time and do more. The receipts reach automatically from the merchants, or you can add them manually. With Esaal you gain full control of your spendings and connect every moment. Features: Smart Receipt that will never fade or get lost, making returns and warranties hassle-free. You can export, share, or print all the information of your receipts at any time for easy reimbursement. Track all your expenses and get 360 views on how much you spend. Search your receipt data by vendor name, the total amount spent, date, and payment type. Access all your electronic receipts from any device anytime, anywhere. Exciting free offers and discounts personalized for your preference. It’s more than a proof of purchase with hyperlinks, product images and promotional offers. Use the real time data to help with cash planning and get 360 view on how much you spend. Easily integrate your in-app barcode with your phone wallet for faster checkout. Esaal app is free to use & download.Having trouble? Please reach out to info@esaal.co
